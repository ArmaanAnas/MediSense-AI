About MediSense AI

MediSense AI is a healthcare diagnosis assistant developed using Flask, MySQL and Machine Learning technologies. The platform helps users evaluate their health status, predict diabetes and heart disease risks, maintain health records, generate reports and receive intelligent health recommendations. The goal is to provide an easy, accessible and AI-powered health monitoring experience.
